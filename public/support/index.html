MostBox Support

Last updated: November 13, 2026

1. Contact

Email dev@most.box for help, content reports, and data requests. You can also file public issues at MostBox Issues; for privacy or security matters, use email or a private GitHub security report and do not post personal data in public issues.

2. Reporting and blocking

In a channel, press and hold a message from another user to report it or block its sender. Report opens an email to dev@most.box with the channel, sender, and message filled in. Block hides that user's messages and alerts in all channels on your device; you can unblock in channel info.

If channel messages, attachments, or most:// links are illegal, infringing, harassing, or otherwise inappropriate, email us the channel name, the related CID or share link, the time, and a short description. We will review reports promptly and reply with the outcome.

MostBox is a peer-to-peer tool: messages and files are stored on participants' own devices, and the project does not operate centralized message or file storage servers, so it cannot remotely delete content already synced to or downloaded by other users. You can leave a channel, delete local files, and stop seeding at any time.

3. Deleting your data

MostBox identities are generated on your device. No account is registered on project servers, and the project does not store your identity, messages, or files. To delete data on your device:

  • delete a file in the app to stop seeding it and remove the local copy;
  • leave a channel in the app to remove it from your contacts and chat list;
  • clear MostBox app data in system settings or uninstall the app to delete all data stored on the device.

If you signed in to a remote node you chose, sign out of the remote node account in the app and delete the corresponding data on that node; remote nodes are managed by their operators. Complete copies already downloaded by other peers and channel messages already sent cannot be recalled from your device.
