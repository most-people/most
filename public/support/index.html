MostBox Support

Last updated: November 22, 2026

1. Contact

Email dev@most.box for help, content reports, and data requests. You can also file public issues at MostBox Issues; for privacy or security matters, use email or a private GitHub security report and do not post personal data in public issues.

2. Reporting and blocking

In a channel, press and hold a message from another user to report it or block its sender. Report opens an email to dev@most.box with the channel, sender, and message filled in. Block hides that user's messages and alerts in all channels on your device; you can unblock in channel info.

If channel messages, attachments, or most:// links are illegal, infringing, harassing, or otherwise inappropriate, email us the channel name, the related CID or share link, the time, and a short description. We will review reports promptly and reply with the outcome.

MostBox is a peer-to-peer tool: messages and files are stored on participants' own devices, and the project does not operate centralized message or file storage servers, so it cannot remotely delete content already synced to or downloaded by other users. You can leave a channel, delete local files, and stop seeding at any time.

3. Delete your MostBox account and data

You can request deletion without signing in, reinstalling MostBox, or connecting to a node. Email dev@most.box with the subject MostBox account and data deletion. Opening an email draft does not send a request; review and send it in your email service. If no email app opens, use your webmail to send to the same address.

Request account and data deletion by email

Include your public account address if available (tap your avatar to open your profile and copy the address), the remote node URL if you used one, and the data you want deleted. If you no longer have these details, describe your situation so we can help identify the relevant data. Never send your password, private key, identity recovery material, device certificate, node invite code, or private channel joining value. We verify ownership before deleting another device's or node's data; we do not ask for secret credentials. We reply with the scope and expected completion time and confirm what has been deleted, what is outside our control, and any data that must be retained and why.

MostBox identities are derived on your device; there is no central account registry that can revoke an identity everywhere. Account deletion involves removing identity credentials and associated data from your devices and the nodes that hold them. Keeping recovery materials or signing in again with the same username and password can recreate that identity. A deletion request does not itself erase your phone or recall copies held by other participants.

Data on your devices

  • Before clearing data, save anything you want to keep. In Android system settings, open Apps → MostBox → Storage → Clear data / Clear storage (wording varies by device). This removes the app's stored identity credentials, node connection history, local chat data, files, transfer records, settings, and runtime logs, and stops local seeding. Repeat on each device you want to erase.
  • Files you exported to Downloads or another location, identity backups, and copies on other devices are separate. Delete those separately if you no longer want to keep them. Do not restore an old app backup if you want the deleted data to stay removed.
  • For selected files, use Files → file details → Delete to remove that app copy and stop seeding its CID. Leaving a channel or deleting its chat list entry is not a complete history wipe. Signing out removes the saved sign-in credentials, but is not account and data deletion.

Remote nodes and support data

Ask the operator of each remote node you used to delete the account's stored data, including account sync registrations and metadata, device authorizations held for sync, channel membership and profile data, messages, and files associated with the account. Stop using account sync on your other devices while the request is handled, so retained copies are not synced back. The project does not operate public remote nodes or have administrator access to independently operated nodes. Contact us if you need help identifying the operator or making a request; we will state which actions require that operator's cooperation. If you cannot access the app or node, you can still start the request by email.

We handle deletion of support correspondence and any account-related data under our control. Support emails are processed by our email provider and remain in the support mailbox until removed; include them in your request if you want them deleted. We do not retain a separate centralized archive of your P2P messages or files. We cannot delete messages or complete files already held by independent peers, or revoke leaked share links. We explain any necessary retention and its duration when responding; we do not describe a partial deletion as deletion of all copies.
