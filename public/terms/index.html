MostBox Terms of Use

Effective: November 13, 2026

1. Nature of the service

MostBox is a P2P channel chat and file transfer tool. It is not a cloud drive, backup service, or permanent storage service. Channel messages are synced only between online peers, and the project does not operate centralized message or file storage servers. Whether a file can be downloaded depends on whether a peer holding a complete copy is online.

2. Your responsibilities

You may only publish, download, send, and share files and messages you have the right to handle, and you agree not to use MostBox to:

  • spread malware, illegal content, or content that infringes intellectual property, privacy, or other rights;
  • bypass the security measures of platforms, devices, or third-party services;
  • harass, threaten, or defraud others, or post hateful, sexual, or violent content, or content that harms minors;
  • disrupt networks or engage in activities prohibited by applicable law.

3. Share links and copies

A most:// link is a capability to access its content. Leaked links cannot be revoked from the P2P network; recipients who finish a download hold a complete copy and continue seeding by default.

4. Mobile app boundaries

Downloads in the mobile app are explicitly started by the user. The app does not install apps, execute downloaded code, or provide a public content directory, and it rejects known app package, script, and executable file types. Background transfers on Android run only after you turn on "Background support" and always show a persistent notification.

5. Availability and data safety

Keep your own copies of important data and verify where files come from. MostBox verifies content identity by CID, but does not guarantee that files are lawful, safe, fit for a particular purpose, or available long term.

6. Contact

Send questions, content reports, and rights complaints to dev@most.box; see MostBox Support for how reports are handled. You can also file at MostBox Issues; use a private GitHub security report for sensitive information.
