MostBox Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

MostBox is a user-operated decentralized channel chat and file transfer tool. Identity and account data are processed on the device, and the app can optionally connect to a remote node chosen by the user. The app contains no ads, no behavioral analytics, and no centralized file storage operated by MostBox.

1. Data on your device

MostBox processes and stores on your device your identity data, channel messages and drafts, files you choose, CIDs, share links, seeding records, transfer status, and basic runtime logs. This data is used for chat, publishing, downloading, integrity checks, resuming seeding, and troubleshooting.

2. P2P network data

When you publish or download a file, the app discovers and connects to other peers through the Hyperswarm network. Connected peers and the distributed network infrastructure may process your IP address, connection times, values derived from CID topics, and other network metadata required to establish connections. File contents travel directly between the peers involved; the MostBox project does not operate centralized file storage servers.

3. Sharing you initiate

When you publish a file or hand it to the system share sheet, the selected file or share link is provided to the recipients you choose. Anyone who knows a most:// link can try to download the content, so protect share links as you would the files themselves.

4. Permissions

  • Internet: discover and connect to P2P peers and transfer files.
  • Local network: on iOS, discover and connect to devices on the same network or P2P network.
  • Network state: detect network changes and restore the node on supported platforms.
  • Wake lock: keep active transfers running on Android.
  • Foreground service: started only when you turn on "Background support" on Android. It shows a persistent notification and helps the local node keep transferring and seeding with the screen off or the app in the background, for up to 6 hours. You can stop it at any time from the app or the notification.
  • System file picker and share sheet: used only when you choose to pick, save, or share files.
  • Camera: requested only when you choose to take a photo, used for that photo attachment.
  • Notifications: requested only when you turn on new message alerts, to show alerts for channel messages received in the background. Notifications do not show message text or attachments.
  • Microphone: requested only when you press and hold to talk, used to record voice messages. Recording in progress is cancelled when the app goes to the background.

Photos are picked with the system picker without full library access. After releasing the talk button you can preview, cancel, or send a voice message; cancelled recordings are deleted from the recording cache. Photos and voice messages are published as CID files and seeded by default, so channel participants, remote nodes you choose, and anyone with the share link can obtain them. MostBox does not request contacts, SMS, call log, location, or full storage access.

5. Remote nodes

You can choose to connect to a remote MostBox node you specify. Once connected, that node processes the file operations, channel operations, and account sync data you initiate on it. Remote nodes are managed by their operators; the MostBox project does not operate public remote nodes.

6. Retention and deletion

Data on your device is kept until you delete the corresponding holding, clear the app data, or uninstall the app. Deleting a local holding stops local seeding, but cannot recall complete copies already downloaded by other peers or invalidate share links that have already leaked. See MostBox Support for how to delete data.

7. Children

MostBox is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Do not use the app to spread illegal, harmful, or infringing content.

8. Changes and contact

This policy is updated when the app's data handling changes. Send privacy questions and data requests to dev@most.box. General questions can also be filed at MostBox Issues; for privacy or security matters, use a private GitHub security report and do not post personal data in public issues.
